1. Introduction

Welcome to WordHeaven ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"), developed and published by PulseCraft Studio. WordHeaven is a 2D anagram crossword puzzle game designed for mental agility, vocabulary enrichment, and relaxation on Android devices.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through transparent data practices. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is processed when you download, install, or play WordHeaven, and how your data is handled in strict compliance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement, the Google Play Families Policy, and international privacy standards.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

WordHeaven is engineered from the ground up as an offline-first game. We do not require, request, access, or store any of the following personal data:

  • No Real Names or Contact Data: We never collect your real name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
  • No Account Credentials: No sign-in or social account linking (Google, Facebook, Apple) is required to play the full campaign.
  • No Financial / Payment Data: We do not collect credit card, bank account, or billing details.
  • No Precise Geolocation: We never request or access GPS or precise location hardware.
  • No Biometric or Device Media Data: We do not access your photos, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage files outside of our own sandboxed application directory.

3. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

To maintain your puzzle progress and unlockable awards across sessions without requiring cloud accounts, the following data is stored locally in sandboxed device memory (via React Native AsyncStorage):

  • Explorer Name: The optional player nickname you type upon first launch is kept exclusively on your local device. It is never uploaded to remote external servers.
  • Gameplay Progression: Completed level numbers (1–100+), star ratings (1 to 3 stars), high scores, and achievement badges unlocked.
  • In-Game Virtual Inventory: Earned coin balances and available hint tokens (coins and hints have zero real-world monetary value).
  • Daily Challenge & Event State: Date stamps for current puzzle streaks and event milestones.
  • Game Settings: Audio mute toggle, sound volume preferences, selected visual theme, and notification opt-in status.

💡 User Control: Because all game data is stored exclusively on your device, you have complete sovereignty over your data. Clearing app data or uninstalling WordHeaven permanently removes all local progress.

5. Advertising Practices & Testing Safety

WordHeaven employs Google AdMob to support ongoing development:

  • Safe Testing Mode: During open testing and beta releases, WordHeaven strictly enforces Google-approved Test Ad Units to protect testers from accidental click penalties.
  • No Inappropriate Content: All ad placements are filtered through Google AdMob content category controls to ensure family-friendly, non-sensitive content.
  • Opting Out of Personalized Ads: Players can opt out of personalized interest-based advertising at any time through their Android device settings:
    Settings → Google → Ads → Reset or Delete Advertising ID.

6. Android System Permissions

WordHeaven requests only the minimum necessary permissions required for functional operation:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Enables AdMob ad delivery and Google Play in-app update checks. Core word puzzles remain 100% playable without an internet connection.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Detects offline status gracefully to prevent unnecessary network queries when disconnected.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Drives subtle haptic feedback vibrations when swiping letters on the anagram wheel.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Enables optional local daily puzzle reminders and streak alerts. Players may decline or toggle notifications off at any time in Settings.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families)

WordHeaven is a general-audience word puzzle game suitable for all ages:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • No personal data collection forms, external web links, chat rooms, or social features exist inside the game.
  • If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will take immediate steps to delete any such data.

8. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA / CPRA)

Under international privacy laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Know & Access: Because we collect zero server-side personal data, all data related to your play is already stored directly on your phone.
  • Right to Delete ("Forget Me"): You can erase all game data instantly by navigating to Android Settings → Apps → WordHeaven → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.

9. Security Measures

We employ sandboxed local storage, proactive Google Play App Signing, and strict release code obfuscation via ProGuard/R8 to protect game binaries and internal logic against unauthorized modification.
