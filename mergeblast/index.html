01. Overview

Welcome to Merge Blast 2048 (the "Game" or "Application"), developed and published by PulseCraft Studio ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are dedicated to maintaining transparent, privacy-first practices across all our mobile games.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored locally on your device, what limited technical data may be processed by integrated third-party services (such as Google AdMob), and how you can exercise your privacy rights when you install and play our games via Google Play.

02. Information We Collect

Our puzzle games are architected to work offline-first with minimal data footprint. PulseCraft Studio does not directly collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, home address, phone number, photos, contacts, or email address during gameplay.

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device (Off-Cloud):

  • Gameplay & Level Progress: High scores, completed levels, puzzle board states, multi-theme unlocks, and undo/hint counters.
  • In-Game Economy & Rewards: Earned coins/gems, daily reward streaks, and unlocked visual customizations.
  • App Preferences: Sound effects, background music, haptic vibration toggles, and display theme settings.

Note: Local game data is saved inside your device's sandboxed application storage (Android SharedPreferences / AsyncStorage) and is never uploaded to PulseCraft Studio servers.

B. Data Automatically Processed by Third-Party SDKs:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID) and App Set ID, subject to your device's permission and consent settings.
  • Coarse Location & Device Specs: IP-based country/region (non-precise), device make/model, OS version, screen resolution, and language locale.
  • Ad Performance & Diagnostics: Ad impressions, rewarded video completions, click events, app launch metrics, and crash stack traces (ANR reports).

04. How Information Is Used

The limited information processed during gameplay is used strictly for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Core Game Functionality: Saving your puzzle progress, achievements, and settings locally so you can resume where you left off.
  • Ad-Supported Free Access: Serving banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video advertisements (e.g., watching an ad to earn extra hints, revives, or gems) so the Game remains free to play.
  • Performance & Crash Diagnostics: Identifying rendering issues, memory bottlenecks, and device-specific bugs across Android versions.
  • Security & Anti-Fraud: Detecting invalid ad traffic and complying with Google Play Developer Program Policies.

06. Consent Management & Ad Personalization

When you launch the Game in regulated regions (including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and applicable US States), we present a certified consent dialog powered by the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK under the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2).

  • Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads: You may choose whether to allow personalized advertisements or opt for non-personalized (contextual) ads.
  • Device-Level Opt-Out: On Android 12, 13, 14, and newer, you can revoke or delete your Advertising ID at any time via Settings → Security & Privacy → More privacy settings → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

07. Android Device Permissions

Our games follow the principle of least privilege and declare only the technical permissions required in AndroidManifest.xml:

Permission Name Android Declaration Why It Is Needed
Internet Access android.permission.INTERNET Required by Google AdMob to request and display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads.
Network State android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Checks whether the device is online before preloading advertisements so gameplay remains smooth offline.
Advertising ID com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID Required on Android 13+ for Google Mobile Ads SDK frequency capping, attribution, and fraud prevention.
Vibration / Haptics android.permission.VIBRATE Provides tactile feedback during puzzle interactions (can be turned off in in-game settings).

The Game never requests access to your Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Call Logs, SMS, or Precise GPS Location.

08. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of young players is paramount to PulseCraft Studio.

  • We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or store any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EEA).
  • Our ad requests are configured to respect age-appropriate content ratings and Google Play Families / Child-Directed treatment flags where applicable.
  • If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has inadvertently submitted personal information to us (for example, via a support email), please contact us at pulsecraft.dev@gmail.com and we will immediately purge such information.

09. Data Storage, Retention & Security

  • Local Data Retention: All local game progress remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the application. Because we do not operate user accounts or cloud databases, there is no personal server-side profile to delete.
  • Encryption in Transit: Any network communication handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS protocols.
  • Android Sandboxing: Local save files are stored inside Android's private application directory, preventing other third-party apps from reading your game state.

10. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, DPDP)

Depending on your jurisdiction—including the European Economic Area (GDPR), California/US States (CCPA/CPRA), and India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act)—you have the right to:

  • Erase Local App Data: Instantly delete all saved scores, progress, and local identifiers by navigating to Android Settings → Apps → Select Game → Storage → Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Opt Out of Targeted Advertising ("Do Not Sell or Share"): Reset or delete your device's Advertising ID in Android Settings, or adjust ad consent preferences inside the app or via youradchoices.com.
  • Lodge an Inquiry: Contact our privacy support team directly with any questions regarding data handling.
