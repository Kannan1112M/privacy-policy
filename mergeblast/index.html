01. Overview

Welcome to Merge Blast (the "Game" or "Application"), published and developed by PulseCraft Games ("we", "our", or "us"). We value your privacy and are committed to maintaining transparent practices regarding information processing.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be accessed, gathered, or utilized when you install and enjoy Merge Blast on Android devices via Google Play. By installing or playing the Game, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

02. Information We Collect

We design our puzzle games to operate with minimal data footprint. We do not collect personally identifying information such as your name, residential address, telephone number, or email address during normal gameplay.

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device:

  • Gameplay Progress: Highest score per puzzle mode (Classic 2048, Drop Merge, Line Connect, Hexa Number Quest), active board state, and undo history.
  • Economy & Cosmetics: Total Gems accumulated, daily mystery gift countdown timers, and unlocked cosmetic avatars and color palettes.
  • Game Settings: Audio toggle, haptic vibration preferences, and high-contrast theme selection.

Note: This data is stored strictly in your device's private app storage using Android SharedPreferences and does not transmit to our servers.

B. Information Automatically Processed by Third-Party SDKs:

  • Device Identifiers: Non-permanent advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID) according to your device's privacy settings.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Operating system version, device model, language setting, coarse geographical area (country/region), and crash reports.
  • Ad Interactions: Metrics related to impressions, clicks, and video completion rates.

03. How Information Is Used

The limited technical information handled during game execution is utilized for:

  • Delivering Core Gameplay: Remembering your unlocked puzzle levels, personal records, and sound/haptic preferences.
  • Displaying Advertisements: Serving contextually appropriate ads to keep Merge Blast free to download and play for all users.
  • Stability & Maintenance: Detecting crashes, fixing memory bottlenecks, and optimizing frame rendering across various Android screen sizes.
  • Compliance & Safety: Preventing automated fraud and upholding Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement standards.

04. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Merge Blast incorporates third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) provided by Google. These providers may collect information according to their respective privacy policies:

Service Provider Purpose Privacy Policy Link
Google AdMob Mobile banner & interstitial ad delivery Google Ads Policy
Google Play Services App distribution, essential runtime APIs & security Google Privacy Policy

💡 Your Ad Choices: You can manage, reset, or delete your Advertising ID on your Android device at any time by navigating to Settings → Google → Ads → Reset or Delete advertising ID.

05. Android Device Permissions

Merge Blast declares only essential permissions in its AndroidManifest.xml to deliver intended gameplay:

Permission Technical Name Reason for Request
Internet Access android.permission.INTERNET Required by Google AdMob to load non-intrusive advertisements.
Vibration / Haptics android.permission.VIBRATE Provides tactile feedback when tiles collide, merge, or achieve high scores (can be toggled in game settings).

The Game does not request sensitive permissions such as Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, or File Storage.

06. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We are committed to protecting the privacy of young players. Merge Blast is designed as a family-friendly number arcade game suitable for all ages.

  • We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age limit in your jurisdiction).
  • Where advertising is displayed, we configure advertising SDKs to comply with Google Play Families Policy requirements and COPPA regulations.
  • If you believe that a minor has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly take appropriate action.

07. Data Storage, Retention & Security

Because game states and gems are stored locally on your device, you maintain full control over your data.

  • Data Retention: Progress remains on your device until you clear the application's storage in Android settings or uninstall the Game.
  • Data Security: We utilize standard Android sandboxing and isolated storage mechanisms to ensure game preferences cannot be accessed by unauthorized applications.

08. Your Rights & Privacy Choices

Depending on your location (such as the European Economic Area under GDPR or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you possess specific privacy rights:

  • Right to Deletion: You can completely erase all stored game records simply by clearing the application storage via Settings → Apps → Merge Blast → Storage → Clear Data or uninstalling the app.
  • Opting Out of Targeted Advertising: You can opt out of personalized ads directly within Android OS settings or via the Digital Advertising Alliance at youradchoices.com.
  • Inquiries & Concerns: You can reach out directly to our team regarding any privacy question.

09. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements, new game modes, or changes in regulatory standards. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this policy occasionally.
